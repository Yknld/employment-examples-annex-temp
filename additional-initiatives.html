Additional employment initiatives — 2025–2026

Ten additional initiative entries relevant to employment equity and multicultural inclusion. Summaries reflect institution-reported practices and results. Some institutions also appear in the initial annex, and broader action plans may encompass related programs; these entries should not be treated as mutually exclusive activities or added together as impact totals.

Federal InstitutionRecipient / BeneficiaryInitiativeCommunities ServedSummary
Fisheries and Oceans CanadaInuit candidates and employees in the Arctic Region2025–2033 Inuit Employment PlanInuitDFO implemented a long-term plan to increase Inuit representation through community outreach, culturally responsive staffing, mentorship, leadership development and Indigenous Career Navigator support. The plan establishes actions addressing complex staffing processes, education and training gaps, housing and childcare pressures, and limited awareness of federal careers.
Canadian Security Intelligence ServiceApplicants and employees facing recruitment and advancement barriersInclusive recruitment, retention and advancement measuresIndigenous, Black and other racialized people; persons with disabilitiesCSIS revised staffing practices through targeted outreach, anonymized résumé screening, culturally relevant analysis of regional hiring and mentorship for diverse employees. Employment equity gap analysis also informed executive staffing decisions. CSIS reported that Indigenous workforce representation increased from 2.3% to 2.4%.
Communications Security Establishment CanadaCandidates and employees from underrepresented groupsInclusive recruitment and expanded Sponsorship ProgramIndigenous and racialized people; neurodivergent employees; persons with disabilities; 2SLGBTQIA+ peopleCSE expanded sponsorship, developed outreach partnerships and used equity dashboards to monitor representation from application to appointment. It established a target that three of every four new hires come from equity-deserving groups. Accessible recruitment tools and leadership-development opportunities supported participation and advancement.
Royal Canadian Mounted PoliceIndigenous and racialized candidates pursuing policing careersIndigenous Pre-Cadet Training Program and Diverse and Inclusive Pre-Cadet ExperienceFirst Nations, Inuit and Métis Peoples; Black and other racialized and underrepresented groupsThe RCMP provided targeted training, mentorship and recruitment support to reduce barriers to policing careers. Through DICE, mentors across the country supported pre-cadets throughout the recruitment process. Two DICE troops were held in 2025–2026.
Canadian Food Inspection AgencyBlack employees across the AgencyAction Plan to Support Black EmployeesBlack employeesCFIA developed a multi-year plan with its Black Employee Network, senior leadership and the People and Culture Branch. Measures include equity objectives in executive performance agreements, disaggregated data to identify advancement gaps, language training, mentorship, sponsorship and culturally informed supports for raising concerns. The plan establishes organization-wide accountability beyond the leadership-development program already selected.
Environment and Climate Change CanadaEmployees and candidates affected by underrepresentation and workplace discrimination2025–2030 Diversity, Equity and Inclusion StrategyUnderrepresented groups, including employees affected by anti-Black racism, anti-Indigenous racism, antisemitism and IslamophobiaECCC launched a five-year strategy containing 18 actions with measurable targets. Its four pillars address equitable hiring, retention and career development; education and engagement; workforce data; and accountability. Developed through employee consultation, the strategy provides a departmental framework for addressing underrepresentation and workplace discrimination.
Canada Revenue AgencyBlack employees seeking leadership opportunitiesBuilding Black Leaders ProgramBlack employeesCRA developed a targeted leadership program to address systemic barriers and gaps in existing leadership-development offerings. The pilot delivered learning, mentorship and networking throughout the year, providing Black employees with development support aligned with the Agency’s leadership and management approach.
Business Development Bank of CanadaEmployees participating in targeted mentorshipCommunities of Focus Mentorship ProgramIndigenous Peoples; Black and other racialized communities; women; persons with disabilities; LGBTQ2+ employeesLaunched in fall 2025, the program expanded on BDC’s Black Professionals Network mentorship pilot. It combines deliberate mentor–mentee matching, structured contact and community-based engagement to broaden access to career development. BDC reported stronger professional connections, engagement, retention and career progression.
Agriculture and Agri-Food CanadaIndigenous students and employees; visible minority employeesIndigenous student recruitment and targeted career-development measuresIndigenous Peoples and visible minority employeesAAFC expanded outreach to Indigenous communities and educational institutions while continuing an official-language training pilot for Indigenous and visible minority employees. Its Lethbridge Research and Development Centre hosted Red Crow Community College agriculture students and promoted Indigenous student recruitment and federal student employment opportunities. These measures supported entry into employment and access to career development.
Defence Research and Development CanadaProspective defence scientists and students from employment equity groupsTargeted Defence Scientist recruitment and Employment Equity student staffing initiativePersons with disabilities and students who self-identify as members of employment equity groupsDRDC launched a partially qualified DS-03 Defence Scientist pool for persons with disabilities and continued targeted student staffing. Hiring managers recorded employment equity considerations in student staffing actions. DRDC reported that the use of employment equity criteria in student staffing increased from approximately 40% in 2024–2025 to approximately 70% in 2025–2026.
